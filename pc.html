How It Works

STEP_01

Budget & Use

You tell me your budget, what games or programs you use, and what you want from the computer.

STEP_02

Parts Quote

I put together a compatible parts list and show you the hardware cost before anything gets ordered.

STEP_03

Parts Payment

You pay the agreed cost of the parts before I order them. Sadly, I do not have a warehouse containing 400 GPUs.

STEP_04

Build & Test

I assemble the PC, manage the cables, configure the basics, install drivers, and test the system.

02 // pricing

Pricing

Hardware and labor are separate. You pay the cost of the parts, plus a separate build/setup fee for assembly and testing. Exact pricing can be added here once I'm ready to take orders.
